# Professional Portfolio

## Professional Summary

*Supply chain and analytics graduate student with extensive leadership experience in logistics, operations, procurement, and project management.*

> Amateurs talk strategy, professionals talk logistics.

I bring more than a decade of logistics and operations leadership experience in the United States Marine Corps, along with hands-on procurement experience in the aerospace and defense industry. My background includes supply chain planning, purchasing, supplier coordination, process improvement, and leading teams in complex operating environments.

I am currently pursuing a Master of Science in Supply Chain Analytics at California State University San Marcos, building on my Bachelor of Science in Global Supply Chain Management.

## Professional Experience

### Supply Chain Procurement Intern — Metrea Aviation

* Executed **73 purchase orders totaling approximately $398,000** in support of aircraft maintenance and operational requirements.
* Supported RFQs, supplier communication, price negotiation, expediting, and delivery tracking.
* Assisted with lead-time management, alternate part sourcing, and supplier documentation.
* Gained experience working within aerospace and defense supply chain requirements and compliance processes.

### Logistics and Operations Leadership — United States Marine Corps

* Served in progressively responsible leadership positions during more than 16 years of military service.
* Directed and coordinated battalion-wide logistics supporting approximately **1,200 personnel across 450+ operational events**, integrating transportation, maintenance, supply, ammunition, fuel, food service, equipment, and field logistics—including support for 990,000 rounds of ammunition, 70,000 meals, 42,500 gallons of fuel, and 165,000+ transportation miles.
* Led teams ranging from small operational crews to organizations of more than 50 Marines.
* Coordinated personnel, equipment, maintenance, transportation, and deployment requirements.
* Used GCSS-MC, an Oracle-based enterprise resource planning system, to support supply, maintenance, and inventory activities.
* Managed logistics activities supporting mission readiness and global operations.
